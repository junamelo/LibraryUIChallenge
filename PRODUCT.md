# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular, as required by the LibraryUI challenge.

## Users

Small shop owners who need a quick view of daily sales, stock pressure, customer orders, and mobile-money payments.

## Product Purpose

Kiosque is a fictional, locally grounded operations dashboard for running a neighbourhood shop from one clear screen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Challenge prototype: all business data is illustrative.
- Must visibly demonstrate at least four LibraryUI-style UI components.
- Must remain useful from mobile through desktop.

## Product Principles

- Make the next operational decision obvious.
- Show local commerce realities without stereotypes.
- Keep dense data calm, legible, and actionable.
- Prefer useful states and clear labels over decoration.

## Evidence on Hand

No brand assets or production data were supplied. Names, transactions, and figures in the prototype are synthetic.
